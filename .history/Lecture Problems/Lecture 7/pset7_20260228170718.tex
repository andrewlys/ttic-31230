\section*{Problem 1. The stationary distribution with minibatching.}
Consider batched SGD defined by:
\[
\Phi -= \eta \hat{g}^B
\]
Where $\hat{g}^B$ is the average of $B$ sampled gradients. 
Let $g = \ee{\hat{g}}$
The covariance matrix at batch size $B$ is given by:
\[
\Sigma^B = \ee{(\hat{g}^B - g)(\hat{g}^{B\top} - g^\top)}
\]
The sde model model of SGD is 
\[
\Phi(t + \Delta t) = \Phi(t) - g\Delta t + \epsilon \sqrt{\Delta t}, \quad \epsilon \sim \cN(0, \eta \Sigma^B)
\]
Show that for $\eta = B \eta_0$ the stationary distribution is determined by $\eta_0$ independent of $B$. 
\begin{proof}
We have the following SDE:
\[
d \Phi_t = - g(\Phi) d t + \sqrt{\eta} (\Sigma^B)^{1/2} d B_t
\]
Let $\cA$ be the infinitessimal generator of this SDE. 
We have:
\[
\cA^\ast f =  \nabla \cdot (g f) + \frac12 \nabla^2 \cdot (f \eta\Sigma^B)
\]
By forward Kolmogorov, we have:
\[
\partial_t p = \cA^\ast p 
\]
To find the stationary distribution, we set $\partial_t p = 0$ and solve the PDE:
\[
\nabla \cdot (g p) + \frac12 \nabla^2 \cdot (p \eta\Sigma^B) = 0
\]

Let's investigate $\Sigma^B$. We have:
\begin{align*}
  \Sigma^B[i,j] &= \ee{(\hat{g}^B[i] - g[i])(\hat{g}^B[j] - g[j])} \\
  &= \Cov{\hat{g}^B[i], \hat{g}^B[j]} \\
  &= \Cov{\frac{1}{B} \sum_{b = 1}^B g[b, i], \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b = 1}^B g[b, j]} \\
  &= \frac{1}{B^2} \sum_{b = 1}^B \sum_{b' = 1}^B \Cov{g[b, i], g[b', j]} \\
  &= \frac{1}{B^2} \sum_{b = 1}^B \Cov{g[b, i], g[b, j]} 
\end{align*}
Where the last line follows because the samples are independent.
Therefore, we have:
\[
\Sigma^B[i,j] = \frac1{B} \left(\frac1{B}\sum_{b = 1}^B \Cov{g[b,i], g[b,j]}\right)
\]
Let $\sigma_{i,j} = \Cov{g[i], g[j]}$. Since the samples are i.i.d. we have $\Cov{g[b,i], g[b,j]} = \sigma_{i,j}$ for all $b$. Therefore, we have:
\[
\Sigma^B[i,j] = \frac1{B} \sigma_{i,j}
\]
Thus, for all $\eta_0$, we have:
\[
\eta \Sigma^B[i,j] = B \eta_0 \frac1{B} \sigma_{i,j} = \eta_0 \sigma_{i,j}
\]
Which has no dependence on $B$. Thus, in the above PDE, the solution has no dependence on $B$.
\end{proof}

\[

\]
